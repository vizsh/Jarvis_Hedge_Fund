# Rural and low-income tools

Five tools for farming and daily-wage households, under the **Rural / ग्रामीण** page and as chat questions (English or Hindi, typed or spoken). All numbers come from code in [`backend/rural.py`](../backend/rural.py); text is hand-written in both languages; nothing is generated by a model.

| Tool | Problem it solves | Ask in chat | API |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moneylender check** (A1) | "₹5 saikda" hides 60% a year. Shows yearly rate, rupees paid, debt-doubling time, and what a group/bank/KCC loan would cost. Modes: interest monthly, all at end, interest-on-interest. | "my sahukar charges 5 rupees per hundred a month on 50000 for 10 months" | `POST /rural/loan` |
| **Is this offer real?** (A2) | "Pay 10,000, get 20,000" schemes, chit funds, pay-to-earn, fake trading groups. 12 red-flag families in English and Hindi plus the implied yearly return; never says "safe", only "no common red flag found". Points to SEBI, RBI Sachet, MCA, 1930. | "pay 10000 get 20000 in 6 months, is this scheme genuine" | `POST /rural/scheme` |
| **My government schemes** (B1) | Eligible-but-unclaimed benefits: 19 schemes (PM-KISAN, KCC, crop insurance, PMJJBY/PMSBY, APY, Ayushman, MGNREGA, e-Shram, PM-SYM, PMAY-G, Ujjwala, pensions, Sukanya, scholarships, Mudra, Vishwakarma, SVANidhi, Jan Dhan, SHG). Each says what you get, who qualifies, where to apply, the official site. Rules are deliberately conservative ("likely"/"maybe"). | "which government schemes can I get" | `POST /rural/entitlements`, `GET /rural/schemes` |
| **Are my papers ready?** (B2) | Applications stall on missing documents. Pick schemes, tick what you have; it names the **one fix that unblocks the most schemes first** (Aadhaar-bank seeding, name mismatch, dormant account) with how to fix each. | "why has my subsidy not come" | `POST /rural/readiness` |
| **Plan my year** (C1) | Income arrives in lumps. Month-by-month balance, the lean months, how much of each lump to keep aside for the months until the next income, and what a moneylender would cost to cover the gap. | "my income comes only after harvest, how do I manage" | `POST /rural/income` |

| **Is my policy good?** (A3) | Endowment / money-back / agent-sold policies sold as "savings". Works out the policy's real yearly return (IRR) from premium, years paid, term and maturity, compares it with the same money in a safe ~7.1% deposit and in today's money, checks cover per rupee, and (if you enter a term quote) shows the term-plan-plus-deposit alternative. Flags how it was sold ("better than an FD", bank staff, pressure, cash to an agent) and detects the **"your old policy has a bonus, pay a fee to release it"** scam. Points to IRDAI Bima Bharosa, the free-look period and the ombudsman. | "my LIC policy premium is 50000 a year for 10 years, maturity 10 lakh after 20 years" | `POST /rural/policy` |
| **UPI safety** (A4) | First-time UPI users lose money to a handful of tricks: "enter your PIN to receive", collect requests, QR sent by a "buyer", "wrong transfer, send it back", AnyDesk, fake customer-care numbers, KYC/electricity threats, small "verification" payments, prize fees, APK links. Tap what is happening (or describe it): it names the trick, says why and what to do, shows the six rules that cover almost every UPI fraud, and points to 1930 and the recovery coach. Includes an 8-scenario **practice quiz** (scenarios and answers shuffled) with explanations and a score. | "a buyer on olx sent a qr code to pay me, scan to receive" | `POST /rural/upi`, `GET /rural/upi/drills` |
| **Why no money?** (B3) | A government payment (PM-KISAN, pension, scholarship, LPG, MGNREGA, ration) did not arrive. It moves through a short chain — registered, approved, Aadhaar linked to one bank account, names match, account active, bank credits — and almost every miss is a break at one link. From what you know it ranks the likely breaks (not registered, paid to another linked account, rejected, not seeded, name mismatch, dormant account, merged-bank IFSC, Aadhaar mobile / e-KYC, plain batch timing), gives the exact fix for each, flags any demand for a fee as fraud or a bribe, points to CPGRAMS, and writes two letters (to the block office and to the bank) with your details filled in. Likelihood scores are rough weights, not probabilities. | "my pm kisan installment is pending" | `POST /rural/dbt` |
| **Sell or hold?** (C3) | The honest version of "mandi price timing": it does **not** predict prices (nobody can, and no free reliable price feed is bundled). It computes the break-even price after storage, shrinkage, handling and the cost of money (a moneylender's 36% makes waiting almost never pay), what holding gains or loses at the price you expect, and — if you paste your own past prices ("Oct 2022: 2100") — how many of those years the price actually rose enough between the same two months, plus a month-by-month index. Verdict needs both your expected price and (if given) most of your own years to clear the bar. | "should I sell 50 quintals of wheat at 2100 now or wait 4 months" | `POST /rural/hold` |
| **Daily saving** (E1) | "₹10 a day" thinking made real. Give a goal (daughter's marriage or education, roof repair, cow, medical fund, festival...), what it costs today and when, and it shows what to put aside each **working day** — with price rises (6% a year) built in — in a recurring deposit versus cash at home, flags a plan that eats more than 15% / 30% of a day's wage and offers a longer one, and lists where to keep it (post-office RD, bank RD, Sukanya for a girl under 10, SHG savings). Or give a daily amount and see what it becomes and how long to reach a target. Four rules (pay yourself first, small emergency pot, never borrow what you can save for, keep it safe). Deposit rates shown are illustrations. | "I want 1 lakh for my daughter's marriage in 5 years, how much a day" | `POST /rural/saving` |
| **Group ledger** (E2) | A self-help group's books. Add members, set the loan interest (% a month) and the loan limit (× savings), then enter each meeting's savings, loans, repayments, fines and withdrawals ("everyone saved ₹500" in one tap). It works out interest on the loan balance per day (repayments pay interest first, then the loan), each member's balance and what they owe, the cash that should be in hand or bank, and each member's **year-end share** of the interest in proportion to savings. It flags a member who stopped repaying or missed saving, a loan above the savings limit, and a group that has lent more than it has. "Can we lend?" checks cash, the member's limit and their record. It writes the **meeting report** and **each member's statement** to copy into WhatsApp, with backup (JSON), CSV export, restore and print. The ledger lives only on the leader's device (its own storage area, untouched by shared-device mode) and is only sent to be calculated, never stored. | "how do I keep the accounts of our self help group" | `POST /rural/shg`, `/rural/shg/lend` |
| **Credit score** (E3) | For first-time borrowers. It does **not** fetch or invent a score (only the four bureaus hold it). It explains what a score is made of, that *no score is not a bad score*, and that repaying a moneylender or chit fund builds none because they do not report. From your answers (borrowing so far, late payments, settled/written-off/unpaid loans, share of card limit used, recent applications, age of oldest account) it says whether your record is likely thin, at risk, fair or healthy, ranks what is helping or hurting with the exact fix for each, and shows what a good record saves in rupees (the same loan at two rates: EMI and total interest). Warns about "score repair" and instant-loan-app traps, points to the free yearly report from each bureau, and drafts the letters to the lender and the bureau to correct a wrong entry. | "why was my loan rejected" | `POST /rural/credit` |

## Honest limits
- **Scheme facts change.** The list carries a review date (`rural.CHECKED`), every screen says to confirm at the place shown, and nobody should pay anyone to apply.
- Eligibility is a guide to *what to ask about*, not a decision; state schemes are not covered beyond a pointer.
- The offer checker measures resemblance to known fraud patterns; it cannot prove an offer honest.
- Spoken dialects (Bhojpuri, Marathi...) are not supported yet; Hindi is.
- Inputs are stored only in the browser's local storage; nothing is sent to a server beyond the calculation request.

## Tests
`tests/test_rural.py` (32): loan figures equal the arithmetic; 5 scam offers flagged and 4 ordinary offers never red; eligibility rules (age windows, tax-payer exclusion, missing bank); every scheme has documents and an official pointer; readiness ordering; income totals; EN and HI questions reach the right tool (Hindi figures are read by code).

## The guide (D1): ask, and it takes you there and does it
Say or type what you want. [`backend/guide.py`](../backend/guide.py) picks the tool, reads what it can from your sentence (English or Hindi figures, read by code), **opens the right page**, asks **only for what is missing, one question at a time** (tappable choices where there are choices), then runs the tool and speaks the result with the page showing it.

- "my sahukar charges 5 rupees per hundred a month" → opens Moneylender check with the rate filled → "How much money did you borrow?" → "50000" → "For how many months?" → "10 months" → page shows 60% a year, and it says so.
- "open the protect page", "take me to the moneylender check", "सुरक्षा पेज खोलिए" navigate (short imperatives only; a long question is answered, not treated as navigation). Any answer that has a page of its own (fee drag, emergency meter, scam coach...) now opens it too, except on the Assistant page where the card is already inline.
- **The assistant stays with you.** The dock (logo, mic, text box) is on every page. While a question is open it shows the question, an example and choice chips; when a task is done it offers "what next" chips. Typing, speaking or tapping all answer the same question. Say "cancel" or press ✕ to stop. Asking a different clear question releases the guide.
- Chained steps carry over: the schemes found in "My government schemes" are used by "Are my papers ready?", which then asks only which papers you have.
- Deterministic, as everywhere: the conversation state lives on the server per browser tab (`cid`); the front end only follows the `guide` payload (`route`, `params`, `ask`, `next`).
- **Also guided:** the fee calculator (once or monthly → amount → years → fee, optional), the emergency meter (savings → monthly spending) and the goal chart (target → monthly amount → years). Their numbers come from the same assistant handlers as typed questions, so the spoken result and the page agree. A vague question ("what does the fund fee cost me") is asked about, not answered with guessed defaults. These pages open at the start and again at the end with the result showing (not at every step, since a half-filled calculator would show misleading defaults).
- Tests: `tests/test_guide.py`.

## Shared-device (kiosk) mode — D3
For a village helper, SHG leader or panchayat desk where one phone or tablet serves many people. Press **👥 Shared** in the top bar (it stays on for that device until you press ✕).
- **Nothing is saved.** Figures typed into the tools and the scam-recovery checklist live in memory only (`frontend/src/lib/pstore.ts`); switching the mode on also deletes anything already saved on the device. Voice and language settings stay (they are the device's, not a person's).
- **Next person** (big green button): stops the voice and mic, wipes the entries, the chat thread and the guide's open question, tells the server to forget that conversation (`POST /kiosk/reset`: guide state, schemes found, chat memory), and returns to the Rural page, empty.
- **Idle timer:** after 3 minutes of no touch (setting `jarvis.kiosk.idle`) a 20-second warning appears ("Clearing for the next person… touch the screen to keep going"), then it wipes the same way.
- **Print my results:** because everything is wiped afterwards, each person can print or save a clean one-page copy of what is on screen (date, results, the "confirm at the place shown / never share OTP" note); navigation, buttons and the assistant box are hidden in print.
- Larger type and touch targets in this mode; a counter shows how many people have been served this session (a number only).
- Limit: it cannot stop someone photographing the screen or reading the browser's history; use a private/guest browser profile on the device if that matters.

## Offline pack — D4
For weak or no signal. On the **Rural** page press **⬇ Download offline pack** once, while online (about 12 MB: the app files, the calculators, and a Python runtime).
- A service worker (`frontend/public/sw.js`, registered only when you press the button) keeps a copy of the app files and serves them with no network; the page itself is network-first so updates still arrive when online.
- The five Rural tools (moneylender check, offer checker, scheme finder, papers, harvest planner) then run **inside the browser**: `backend/rural.py` itself is executed by Pyodide (Python compiled to WebAssembly) — not a re-write in another language — so offline answers equal server answers, in English and Hindi. `GET /offline/rural.py` serves that file; `GET /offline/assets` lists what to save.
- The page shows "No internet right now · these tools work without internet" and keeps working; typed Hindi/English forms and results are unchanged.
- Not available offline: the voice assistant and guided conversation (they run on the server), stock research, portfolio pages, WhatsApp/SMS. The Rural page's own forms are the offline way to do the same tasks.
- Tests: `tests/test_offline.py` (rural.py is standard-library only and gives identical results when run standalone).
- Verified: the in-browser Python engine, all five tools offline (with the connection reported as down). Not verifiable in the built-in preview browser: service-worker registration (that pane refuses it). Please try the full "download, then disconnect" path in Chrome or Edge.
