# Rural and low-income tools

Five tools for farming and daily-wage households, under the **Rural / ग्रामीण** page and as chat questions (English or Hindi, typed or spoken). All numbers come from code in [`backend/rural.py`](../backend/rural.py); text is hand-written in both languages; nothing is generated by a model.

| Tool | Problem it solves | Ask in chat | API |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moneylender check** (A1) | "₹5 saikda" hides 60% a year. Shows yearly rate, rupees paid, debt-doubling time, and what a group/bank/KCC loan would cost. Modes: interest monthly, all at end, interest-on-interest. | "my sahukar charges 5 rupees per hundred a month on 50000 for 10 months" | `POST /rural/loan` |
| **Is this offer real?** (A2) | "Pay 10,000, get 20,000" schemes, chit funds, pay-to-earn, fake trading groups. 12 red-flag families in English and Hindi plus the implied yearly return; never says "safe", only "no common red flag found". Points to SEBI, RBI Sachet, MCA, 1930. | "pay 10000 get 20000 in 6 months, is this scheme genuine" | `POST /rural/scheme` |
| **My government schemes** (B1) | Eligible-but-unclaimed benefits: 19 schemes (PM-KISAN, KCC, crop insurance, PMJJBY/PMSBY, APY, Ayushman, MGNREGA, e-Shram, PM-SYM, PMAY-G, Ujjwala, pensions, Sukanya, scholarships, Mudra, Vishwakarma, SVANidhi, Jan Dhan, SHG). Each says what you get, who qualifies, where to apply, the official site. Rules are deliberately conservative ("likely"/"maybe"). | "which government schemes can I get" | `POST /rural/entitlements`, `GET /rural/schemes` |
| **Are my papers ready?** (B2) | Applications stall on missing documents. Pick schemes, tick what you have; it names the **one fix that unblocks the most schemes first** (Aadhaar-bank seeding, name mismatch, dormant account) with how to fix each. | "why has my subsidy not come" | `POST /rural/readiness` |
| **Plan my year** (C1) | Income arrives in lumps. Month-by-month balance, the lean months, how much of each lump to keep aside for the months until the next income, and what a moneylender would cost to cover the gap. | "my income comes only after harvest, how do I manage" | `POST /rural/income` |

## Honest limits
- **Scheme facts change.** The list carries a review date (`rural.CHECKED`), every screen says to confirm at the place shown, and nobody should pay anyone to apply.
- Eligibility is a guide to *what to ask about*, not a decision; state schemes are not covered beyond a pointer.
- The offer checker measures resemblance to known fraud patterns; it cannot prove an offer honest.
- Spoken dialects (Bhojpuri, Marathi...) are not supported yet; Hindi is.
- Inputs are stored only in the browser's local storage; nothing is sent to a server beyond the calculation request.

## Tests
`tests/test_rural.py` (32): loan figures equal the arithmetic; 5 scam offers flagged and 4 ordinary offers never red; eligibility rules (age windows, tax-payer exclusion, missing bank); every scheme has documents and an official pointer; readiness ordering; income totals; EN and HI questions reach the right tool (Hindi figures are read by code).
