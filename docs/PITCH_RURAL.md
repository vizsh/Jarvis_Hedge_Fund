# Pitching Jarvis to rural India

## The one-line pitch
"Your money questions answered in Hindi, on the phone you already have, free, and it tells you when someone is cheating you."

## Who it is for, and what each one gets
| Person | Their problem | What they get on the phone |
|---|---|---|
| Farmer / daily-wage family | Moneylender quotes "5 rupees per 100", true cost unknown | The yearly rate and rupees paid, and a cheaper option |
| SHG woman | Group accounts, interest and fines are hard to keep | Group ledger, member statements, meeting report |
| Pensioner / beneficiary | Subsidy or pension "has not come" | Why it is stuck and the exact fix |
| Anyone with a loan offer, policy or "double your money" scheme | Pressure to sign now | Red-flag check with reasons, and what to do |
| Anyone who got a scam call | Panic, OTP already read out | The first hour, step by step, and 1930 |

## How it works for them (one minute, no app)
1. Send "hi" on WhatsApp (or SMS on a basic phone) to the Jarvis number.
2. Speak a voice note in Hindi: "sahukar 5 rupaye sainkda, 50 hazar".
3. Get the answer in Hindi with the working, and tap a number to go deeper.

## The 6-minute demo that holds attention
1. **Open with their problem, not our product (0:00-0:30).** Ask the room: "Who here has ever been quoted a monthly rate?" Then say the number out loud.
2. **Show the loan (0:30-1:45).** Rural page, moneylender check, Hindi. Let the yearly figure land on screen.
3. **Show the scam (1:45-2:45).** Assistant: "someone asked for my OTP on a call". Read the answer back in Hindi.
4. **Show WhatsApp live (2:45-4:15).** Hand the phone to someone in the room. Ask them to send a voice note. Their answer appears in the chat as Hindi text. This is the moment people trust it.
5. **Show the proof (4:15-5:15).** Govern: one blocked trade and the Tamper test. "Rules nobody can argue with, and a record that cannot be edited."
6. **Close with the ask (5:15-6:00).** "Which of your members would use this first? Can we run it with them for a month?"

## Objections and answers
- *"Is this financial advice?"* No. It describes and explains. It never says buy, sell or hold.
- *"Does it work without internet?"* The rural tools run offline once the pack is saved. Prices need a connection.
- *"What if it is wrong?"* Numbers come from calculators and stored data, never a language model, and every answer shows its working.
- *"Who pays?"* Users do not. Banks, co-operatives, NGOs and CSR funds pay for fewer frauds and better reach.

## Who to pitch to first
- A self-help-group federation (ready users, trusted by members)
- A co-operative or small finance bank's outreach team
- A Common Service Centre operator (shared device, footfall)
- A state rural-livelihood mission or an NGO working on financial literacy

## Pilot offer (what to put in the room)
One village or one SHG federation, 8 weeks, a shared WhatsApp number, weekly 20-minute sessions, and three measures: borrowers who learn the true yearly rate, scams refused, schemes found.
