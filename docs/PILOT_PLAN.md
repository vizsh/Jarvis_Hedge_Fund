# Pilot plan

A pilot is the only way to turn the assumptions in [IMPACT_MODEL.md](IMPACT_MODEL.md) into facts. It is short, bounded, and agreed in writing before it starts.

## 1. Purpose

Measure, in one district or one set of bank branches, whether the guided first hour, the credit-cost check and the offline pack change outcomes. Produce the counts that replace the assumptions.

## 2. Shape

| Item | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Partner | One NGO or one co-operative bank with branches in one district |
| Duration | 3 to 6 months |
| Scope | Assistant (web and WhatsApp or SMS), first-hour recovery coach, credit-cost check, offline pack for field staff |
| Users | Staff first, then customers who choose to use it |
| Price | Nominal or free, with a conversion to a licence only if the agreed outcome is met |
| Data | Only the counts in section 4. No personal data leaves the partner's control |

## 3. Before the start (must be signed)

1. Written outcome measures and how they are counted (section 4).
2. A baseline period of the same length, taken from the partner's records, so the before and after are comparable.
3. Data protection terms: who holds what, consent for any customer contact, and how to delete data.
4. A named contact on the partner side who can approve Hindi wording and field use.
5. Agreement that a negative result is reported as such.

## 4. Outcome measures

| Measure | How it is counted | Replaces assumption |
|---|---|---|
| Fraud complaints per 1,000 customers | Partner's complaint log, same months a year apart | 2 per 1,000 |
| Staff hours and cost per complaint | Time records and a fixed staff-cost rate | ₹1,500 and 20% |
| Money lost and money recovered per case | Case files | ₹50,000 and 5% stopped |
| Cases where the recovery steps were used in the first hour | Recovery coach session log (counts only) | Not yet measured |
| Loans moved from informal lenders to formal credit | Loan records | 0.5 point and 50% |
| Repayment of moved loans, against a comparison group | Loan records | Not yet measured |
| Conversations per customer and their cost | Messaging provider bill | 2 a year and ₹0.80 |
| Customer questions the assistant could not answer | Unanswered-question log (counts and topics only) | Not yet measured |

The product must count only what the pilot needs. Logs store counts and topics, not personal details.

## 5. Phases

| Phase | Weeks | Work | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Agreement | 0 to 4 | Sign the measures, the baseline, the data terms | Signed pilot agreement |
| 1. Setup | 4 to 8 | Hindi review by partner staff; set up the offline pack; train field staff | Working pilot in one location |
| 2. Live | 8 to 20 | Staff and customers use the tools; monthly counts reviewed | Monthly report |
| 3. Read-out | 20 to 24 | Compare with the baseline; write the results honestly | Pilot report with measured values |

## 6. Decision points

| After the read-out | Next step |
|---|---|
| Savings measured and above the proposed fee | Draft a licence or programme contract using the measured values |
| Savings measured and below the fee | Reprice, narrow the scope, or stop; report it |
| Savings not measurable | Improve the counts and run a second, longer pilot; do not sell on forecasts |
| Customers do not use it | Find out why in the field before changing the product |

## 7. Costs of the pilot

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Hosting | ₹2,000 a month (planning range) |
| Messaging | Actual provider cost; VERIFY current rate card |
| Field support | Staff time from the team (not yet costed) |
| Legal review of the agreement | Not yet costed |
| Translation review | Partner staff time |

## 8. What the pilot must not do

- It must not give advice to buy, sell or hold any investment.
- It must not take fees from customers.
- It must not present forecasts as results.
- It must not use any figure from [BUSINESS_DATA_COSTS.md](BUSINESS_DATA_COSTS.md) section 3 that has not been checked against its source.

## 9. What to bring to a second conversation

1. The measured counts, with the baseline next to them.
2. The assumptions that were confirmed and the ones that were not.
3. The list of known limits from [README.md](../README.md#honest-limits).
4. A proposed licence or contract built only from measured values.
